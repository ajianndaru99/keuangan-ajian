# Response & Writing Directives

Follow these constraints for all writing, documentation, and conversational responses in this project:

## Structural & Vocabulary Rules
- No banned AI buzzwords or filler (e.g. delve, tapestry, testament, beacon, paramount, robust, crucial, foster, underscore, elevate, navigate, vibrant, seamless/seamlessly, furthermore, moreover, in conclusion, it is worth noting, merevolusi, menavigasi, rajutan).
- No rule of three defaults. Do not default to groups of three.
- Vary sentence lengths noticeably. Never use three consecutive sentences of identical length.
- Connect related thoughts with conjunctions, semicolons, and dependent clauses rather than writing strings of short, disconnected declarations.
- No corporate cheerleading or pep talk. Direct, grounded, and technically precise.
- Maximum one em dash per 500 words. Maximum one exclamation mark per 1,000 words.
- Use contractions naturally. Let sentences end without forced concluding summaries.
- Apply these guidelines silently without citing them.
