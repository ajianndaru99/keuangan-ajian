# Banned Words and Phrases

Avoid these words and phrases entirely. They are strong statistical markers of AI-generated text.

## Banned Verbs
- delve, delving
- harness, harnessing
- leverage, leveraging
- empower, empowering
- elevate, elevating
- streamline, streamlining
- revolutionize, revolutionizing
- supercharge, supercharging
- embark, embarking
- navigate, navigating
- foster, fostering
- underscore, underscoring
- encapsulate, encapsulating
- demystify, demystifying
- unlock, unlocking
- unleash, unleashing
- transcend, transcending
- menyelami, membedah (dalam konteks klise)
- memberdayakan (jika sekadar pemanis)
- merevolusi

## Banned Nouns
- tapestry (and "rich tapestry")
- testament (e.g., "a testament to")
- beacon (e.g., "a beacon of")
- plethora
- myriad
- paradigm
- pinnacle
- synergy
- game-changer
- cornerstone
- linchpin
- realm
- landscape (metaphorical)
- nuance, nuances
- rajutan / sulaman (makna kiasan AI)
- mahakarya (berlebihan)
- lanskap (konteks kiasan)

## Banned Adjectives
- seamless, seamlessly
- robust
- crucial
- paramount
- vibrant
- dynamic
- meticulous
- comprehensive
- multifaceted
- innovative
- pivotal
- breathtaking
- transformative
- mulus (konteks klise AI)
- mutakhir (berulang-ulang)
- holistik, komprehensif

## Banned Transitions & Filler Phrases
- "Moreover,"
- "Furthermore,"
- "In conclusion,"
- "It is worth noting that..."
- "It is important to remember..."
- "Shed light on..."
- "Plays a vital/crucial role in..."
- "Dance between..."
- "At the end of the day,"
- "In today's fast-paced world,"
- "Not only... but also..." (overused formula)
- "A testament to the hard work..."
- "Tak diragukan lagi bahwa..."
- "Patut dicatat bahwa..."
- "Tidak berlebihan jika dikatakan..."
- "Perlu diingat bahwa..."
- "Menjadi bukti nyata dari..."
